Frequently asked

Straight answers. No AI theatre.

01 What can an AI agent actually do?+

An agent can understand requests, make decisions within defined rules, use connected tools and complete actions—such as answering a call, checking availability, creating a booking, updating a CRM or escalating to a person.

02 Will it work with our existing systems?+

Usually, yes. We integrate with common CRMs, calendars, phone systems, messaging tools, databases and internal platforms through native integrations or APIs.

03 How do you prevent incorrect responses?+

We constrain the agent to approved knowledge, define explicit boundaries, test edge cases, add confidence thresholds and route sensitive or uncertain situations to a human.

04 Is this only for large enterprises?+

No. A local clinic or restaurant can start with one focused agent, while an enterprise can deploy multiple specialised agents across teams and systems.

05 How quickly can we launch?+

A focused use case can often be piloted within a few weeks. Complex, regulated or multi-system deployments require a longer discovery, integration and testing cycle.
